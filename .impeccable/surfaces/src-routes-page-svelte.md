# Amber: the list (whole app surface)

Scope: the one page at `/` and its sheets. Mode: Operate. Audience: the general public, on a phone first, keeping everyday to-dos and a bucket list that lives only in their browser.

Job: add, tick and find tasks in seconds; keep dreams, and see the ones achieved.

Constraints: Rhodonite colors only (graphite, one pastel pink for the primary action and focus); Plus Jakarta Sans, Itrium's brand face; WCAG 2.2 AA; 44px touch targets; no inline styles; nothing loaded from another domain. Standard web controls throughout. Light is entirely light and dark entirely dark: no dark-filled surface in light mode (the owner's rule, 18 November 2026).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Amber is a specimen cabinet whose open drawer is the list: the drawer you choose slides out of the cabinet and becomes the tray you work in, one continuous surface. It refuses the category default of a sidebar of links beside a floating page, and the two-tone dark-sidebar shell.

OWN-WORLD: Light: a pale cabinet body (one step under the page) holding its drawers as plain muted labels with framed counts, a front showing only under the pointer; the open drawer alone takes the white of the tray, semibold in graphite, and runs straight into it as one surface with no seam and no outline, the tray set off the near-white page by a pale hairline. Dark: the same shapes in graphite tones, the tray set off by its tone alone, no outline either. Slips are ruled rows in hairline trays inside the drawer; framed labels in list colors; the Achieved shelf of specimens; pink only on Add and focus. Plus Jakarta Sans; 6–8px corners; no shadows at rest.

STORY: The visitor pulls out a drawer, sees it open into its tray, adds a slip, ticks it; a dream achieved is filed on the shelf as a specimen with its date.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with Amber's icon, wordmark and the wide search bar. On a wide screen, the cabinet body at left with a drawer per place; the open one, in the tray's tone, runs level into the tray filling the rest: the place's heading, quick add, then its sections. On a phone, the drawers sit in the Lists sheet, and the open place's name is a tab on top of the tray, in its tone, carrying its count.

FORM: Graphite cabinet (Impeccable's pick, position 1 on the ordered list; the roll assigned the herbarium sheet at 3), steered by the owner to one continuous light or dark world with no two-tone split. seed:132707f1f825a01a

SIGNATURE MOVE: The open drawer is the tray: choosing a place pulls its drawer out into the work area, the drawer and the tray one surface in one tone, with no seam and no outline. Achieving a dream still files it on the Achieved shelf as a specimen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
